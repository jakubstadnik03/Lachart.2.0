🔁 The loop

Plan it, ride it, then take it apart.

A lactate test is a snapshot — the point is what the next three months of training do to it. LaChart runs the whole loop, end to end, and every number comes off your own curve instead of a percentage of a guess.

1

Connect

Strava, Garmin or Apple Health — rides, runs and swims arrive on their own with power, pace, HR, cadence and laps. Or drop a .FIT file straight in.

2

Plan

Build the week in the calendar or a whole season in the annual plan. Structured intervals with steps, ramps and repeats, saved as templates and pushed to your watch.

3

Train

The session is on your watch with the zones from your own curve. Tick it off and add RPE, a lactate value or a comment while it's still fresh.

4

Analyse

Laps, streams, peak curves, time in zones, load and form — and the same session against every previous attempt at it.
